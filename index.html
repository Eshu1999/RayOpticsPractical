Ray Bench

Place an object in front of a mirror or lens and watch the rays form an image. Drag the object, or change any value in the panel.

Drag the arrow sideways to change its distance, or drag its tip to change its height. Light travels left to right.

Type any number, or inf and -inf, in the boxes. A negative distance puts a virtual object behind the element, and a negative focal length flips a converging element to diverging. Sliders cover a shorter range.
